Notes

c-icap
Current AV failure mode is fail-{{ clamav_options.clamav_fail_mode }}. Fail-open renders Squid bypass=on and c-icap virus_scan.PassOnError on so browsing continues unscanned during scanner outages; fail-closed renders bypass=off and PassOnError off so affected transfers fail instead. The download streaming helper follows the same mode. CLAMAV_REQUIRED=1 on the proxy forces fail-closed.
Upload and download scanning are independently configurable above. Enable/Disable scanning switches both together and leaves the rest of the policy alone; it does not start or stop clamd or the ICAP services.
Uploads are scanned by c-icap virus_scan in the proxy container. Downloads use the same service when clamd is local, and a streaming INSTREAM helper when CLAMD_HOST is remote. Both send data to clamd at CLAMD_HOST:CLAMD_PORT.
Download scanning covers complete GET/HEAD response bodies. Range requests, 206 partial responses, and Server-Sent Events (request Accept names text/event-stream and the reply is text/event-stream) are not sent to RESPMOD because they are partial or unbounded; URL-extension file-security blocks still apply to them. Responses larger than CLAMAV_STREAM_MAX_BYTES (256 MiB by default) are never labelled clean: fail-open delivers them unscanned, fail-closed blocks them.
With a remote clamd, downloads are delivered while they are scanned: only the newest part of a response (at least 256 KiB and at least 1% of its length; 1 KiB for HTML and JavaScript, so pages render while they load) is held back until ClamAV's verdict. SVG, PDF and responses without a usable Content-Type are held whole until the verdict instead. Small infected files get the 403 block page; a larger infected download is cut off before that held-back part, so the browser reports the download as failed and the file is never complete. Squid does not cache the aborted response.
